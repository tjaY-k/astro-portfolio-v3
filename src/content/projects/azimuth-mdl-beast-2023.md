Directed video technical management, projection mapping, and disguise programming for AZIMUTH, a two-day desert music festival staged in AlUla, Saudi Arabia — grounds that form the country's first UNESCO World Heritage Site, surrounded by historical ruins dating to the 6th century BC. The festival runs from dusk until dawn and drew an estimated 200,000+ attendees. Creative visuals were developed by FRAY Studio with Cassius Creative using Notch, with over 20 unique real-time looks designed to respond live to music, lighting and stage action — requiring playback infrastructure that could run flexibly for undefined durations through the night.
