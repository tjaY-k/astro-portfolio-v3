Programmed pre-game shows and operated media servers live for the 2021 FIBA Basketball Champions League Final 8 (5–9 May 2021) at the Trade Union Sport Palace / Nagorny arena in Nizhny Novgorod — the concluding phase of the BCL's fifth season, contested by eight clubs from six countries in a single-game knockout format. Billed by organisers as the largest international sports tournament hosted by the city since the 2018 FIFA World Cup. Delivered visual intros, player highlights, and real-time arena graphics for both the live crowd and the international broadcast feed. San Pablo Burgos took the title, becoming the competition's first back-to-back champion.
