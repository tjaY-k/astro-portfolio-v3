Directed video technical operations for the Dubai World Cup at Meydan Racecourse — widely billed as the world's most spectacular race day, with $30.5 million in prize money across nine races and a $12 million purse for the feature event. The 29th edition in 2025 drew a record-breaking crowd of over 65,000 (up 18% from 2024) and was broadcast to more than 170 countries across 37 networks. Work covered flawless screen integration across the venue for both the live crowd and the international broadcast feed, alongside the race day's closing ceremony, concert, and fireworks programme.
