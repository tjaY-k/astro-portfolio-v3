Served as Technical Director on behalf of MediaPro for client Artnovi, delivering overall technical direction — a step up in scope from a project video technical manager role — for the UAE's 54th Eid Al Etihad (National Day) celebration at the historic Al Dhafra Fort. The video scope centred on large-scale 3D projection mapping across a 50-metre surface of the fort, mapped to the building's real geometry rather than a flat canvas. The wider celebration also featured fireworks and traditional cultural performances, and was attended by members of Abu Dhabi's ruling family, including Sheikh Sultan bin Hamdan bin Zayed Al Nahyan and Sheikh Yas bin Hamdan bin Zayed Al Nahyan.
