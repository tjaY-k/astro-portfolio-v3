Served as Technical Director on behalf of MediaPro for client Artnovi, delivering overall technical direction — a step up in scope from a project video technical manager role — for the UAE's 54th Eid Al Etihad (National Day) celebration at the historic Al Dhafra Fort. The production combined large-scale 3D projection mapping across a 50-metre surface with a drone performance, in which drone-formed hands transitioned into the UAE flag rendered through 3D mapping, alongside fireworks and traditional cultural performances. The celebration was attended by members of Abu Dhabi's ruling family, including Sheikh Sultan bin Hamdan bin Zayed Al Nahyan and Sheikh Yas bin Hamdan bin Zayed Al Nahyan.
