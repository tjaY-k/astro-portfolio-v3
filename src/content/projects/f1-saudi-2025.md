Managed immersive fanzone projection mapping (2022) and video operations with disguise programming for the opening and closing ceremonies (2023–2025) at the Formula 1 STC Saudi Arabian Grand Prix, held at the Jeddah Corniche Circuit — the longest and fastest street circuit on the F1 calendar at 6.174 km with an average lap speed of 252 km/h. The Grand Prix draws crowds in excess of 140,000 across the race weekend and feeds a global F1 broadcast audience spanning more than 200 countries, including a US TV audience of 1.5 million viewers.
