Broadcast Stadium Manager at Mordovia Arena (44,000 capacity) during the 2018 FIFA World Cup. Supervised installation of all broadcast structures including Broadcast Compound, camera platforms, announce positions, commentary positions and Presentation Studios in full compliance with FIFA standards.

Managed CATV infrastructure, giant video screen operations, infotainment programme and coordination with IT&T teams. Ensured seamless broadcast feed delivery and technical support across the venue's four group-stage matches — Peru–Denmark, Colombia–Japan, Iran–Portugal and Panama–Tunisia — each played to a full house and carried worldwide off the tournament's host feed.
