Configured the xR studio setup, managed technical operations, and worked alongside camera operators for on-air broadcasts in a virtual extended reality environment — IBM Russia's Cloud and AI Forum, staged in November 2020. The build combined LED walls with camera tracking systems to create seamless virtual sets, enabling interactive and immersive delivery of corporate presentations and product demos at a time when the pandemic had made virtual production a critical channel for enterprise events.

stYpe RedSpy fed tracking data from three cameras — one of them a 20-metre telescopic crane — into the media servers, with the real-time graphics running in Notch. Produced by dreamlaser for G&G Marketing Communications and IBM Russia; Notch published the show as a case study.
