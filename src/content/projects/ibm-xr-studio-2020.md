Configured the xR studio setup, managed technical operations, and worked alongside camera operators for on-air broadcasts in a virtual extended reality environment for IBM. The build combined LED walls with camera tracking systems to create seamless virtual sets, enabling interactive and immersive delivery of corporate presentations and product demos at a time when the pandemic had made virtual production a critical channel for enterprise events.
