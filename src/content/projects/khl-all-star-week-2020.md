Oversaw technical management for the FONBET 2020 KHL All-Star Week in Moscow (18–19 January 2020) at VTB Arena — the Kontinental Hockey League's flagship showcase weekend, combining the Master Show skills competition with the All-Star Game, contested as a mini-tournament between teams representing the league's four divisions and featuring its highest-profile players. Coordinated video systems, screens, and media servers to deliver pre-game shows, player introductions, and in-arena visuals across both event days.
