Handled show programming and projection mapping for the gala dinner staged around the Formula 1 Monaco Grand Prix — the most prestigious race weekend on the F1 calendar, and an event whose surrounding hospitality programme draws royalty, team principals, drivers, and high-net-worth guests. Custom visuals were projected onto the venue's surfaces to create an elegant, themed atmosphere blending motorsport imagery with sophisticated lighting design.
