Programmed projection mapping at the Polytech festival location using disguise (d3), plus LED bars at the Ostankino site, for the Moscow International Festival "Circle of Light" — one of the largest events of its kind in Europe, which transforms landmark buildings and public spaces across the city into large-scale light art for several nights each autumn, drawing crowds in the hundreds of thousands.
