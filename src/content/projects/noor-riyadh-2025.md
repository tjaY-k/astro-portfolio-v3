Served as projection technical director for seven sites, from projection study and calculations through to leading the team and supervising delivery on site, on projectors supplied by MediaPro — the facades of the stc Metro Station and the Zaha Hadid-designed KAFD Metro Station, Qasr Al Hokm District, and several smaller sites within the King Abdulaziz Historical Center complex — at the fifth edition of Noor Riyadh (20 November – 6 December 2025), the world's largest light art festival, presented by Riyadh Art under the Royal Commission for Riyadh City as part of Saudi Vision 2030. The 2025 edition featured 60 artworks by 59 artists from 24 countries, including more than 35 new commissions, under the theme 'In the Blink of an Eye'. Works spanned six locations across the city: Qasr Al Hokm District, King Abdulaziz Historical Center, stc Metro Station, the Zaha Hadid-designed KAFD Metro Station, Al Faisaliah Tower, and JAX District. Since 2021 the festival has drawn more than 9.6 million visitors.
