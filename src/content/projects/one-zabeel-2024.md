Led the projection study, mapping, and disguise media server programming for the Grand Opening of One&Only One Za'abeel, Dubai's first urban vertical resort. The projection spanned both One Za'abeel skyscrapers and The Link — the 230-metre cantilevered structure suspended 100 metres above ground — running across three nights of the launch celebration. Comprehensive laser scanning of the building's north face was integrated as 3D data into disguise Cloud, with pre-production planning done in Mapping Matter; the team had just two months from scan completion to delivery. The show ran on 165 Panasonic RQ35k projectors driven by 6 disguise VX 4+ media servers and a single GX 2C, and was awarded three Guinness World Records: longest projection mapping in the world, largest architectural temporary projection mapping, and largest mesh screen projection. Delivered for event manager Bureau Beatrice.
