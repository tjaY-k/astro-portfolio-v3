Handled show programming and LED bars programming using MadMapper for the opening ceremony of the XXIX Russian Student Spring national festival (15–20 May 2021, Nizhny Novgorod) — the national final of Russia's largest student arts programme, which involves roughly 1.5 million students from over 1,200 educational institutions annually. The 2021 edition was staged as the first major show in Nizhny Novgorod's 800th anniversary programme, with the ceremony broadcast live to a public screen on Minin and Pozharsky Square in the city centre.
