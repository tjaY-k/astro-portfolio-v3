Oversaw video technical management for the World Trade Organization's 13th Ministerial Conference at ADNEC, Abu Dhabi (26 February – 2 March 2024). The conference convened nearly 4,000 ministers, senior trade officials and delegates from the WTO's 164 members and observers, plus civil society, business and global media. Work covered multi-screen setups and live feeds across opening and closing ceremonies, plenary sessions, ministerial conversations, and concurrent side events — a high-stakes diplomatic environment where the conference was extended by an additional day mid-event, requiring the video systems to absorb a live schedule change.
